Save a video

Pulls the video or photo out of a Facebook post — including posts in private groups you’re a member of — and saves it as a file you can attach to a Trello card.

Found on that post

One-time set-up

1
Show your bookmarks bar if it’s hidden: ⌘⇧B on a Mac, Ctrl⇧B on Windows.
2
Drag this button up onto the bar. Don’t click it here — it only does something on a Facebook page.
Save this video
That’s it. You only ever do this once.

Every time you want a video

1
Open the Facebook post so the video is on screen, and let it start playing for a second.
2
Click Save this video in your bookmarks bar.
3
This page opens with the file listed. Pick Download.
The bookmark didn’t find anything — what now?

Two things usually fix it. First, open the video on its own page rather than in the group feed (click the video, or its timestamp, so the address bar shows a single post). Second, let it play for a couple of seconds before clicking the bookmark.

Still nothing? Paste the page’s source instead: right-click a blank part of the Facebook page → View page source, select all (⌘A), copy (⌘C), and drop it here.

Paste a link instead (only works for public posts)

A link on its own is enough only when the post is public. Anything in a private group has to come from the bookmark or the page source, because this site can’t see inside your Facebook account — and you should never give a website your Facebook password to make it try.

Only save things you’re allowed to keep a copy of.
